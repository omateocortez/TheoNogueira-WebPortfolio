about the practice

stories shaped through atmosphere, gesture and emotional precision.

Theo Nogueira is a Brazilian director and writer interested in films where image carries emotional weight as strongly as dialogue. His work lives between narrative cinema, branded direction, and projects still developing on the page.

He is drawn to stories that feel intimate without losing formal clarity, building visual worlds through rhythm, framing, and close attention to performance. Across formats, his aim is to create work that feels tactile, cinematic, and deeply human.

This portfolio gathers selected work, visual references, and ongoing directions that reflect his current language as a filmmaker.
