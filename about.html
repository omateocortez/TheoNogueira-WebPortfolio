theo nogueira

Theo Nogueira is a Brazilian film director interested in image-led storytelling, atmosphere, and emotional precision.

His work moves across narrative, branded films, and music-driven pieces, always shaped by a strong sense of rhythm and visual composition. He is drawn to projects where gesture, silence, and framing carry as much weight as dialogue.

Across productions, Theo develops films that balance clarity with texture, building visual worlds that feel intimate, cinematic, and memorable. His approach stays close to performance while creating frames with distinct tone and movement.

This portfolio gathers a selection of ongoing work, references, and collaborations that reflect his current visual language and the direction of his practice.
