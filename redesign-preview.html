Theo Nogueira is a director and writer based in São Paulo, working at the intersection of narrative cinema and image-led commercial work. Trained in the grammar of composition and performance, he approaches each project as an act of attention — to light, to space, to the weight of a glance.

His practice is built around the belief that the most honest cinema lives in the small gestures: the pause before a word, a hand left on a table, a room that holds more than its inhabitants. These are the moments he is after.

This portfolio represents the directing and writing work that is defining his practice — films still developing, commissions completed, and frames that felt true.
