the practice

a director working at the intersection of narrative and image-led commercial cinema.

Theo Nogueira is a filmmaker and writer based in São Paulo. His practice is shaped by atmosphere, gesture, and emotional precision — an attention to the small moments that carry the weight of a scene.

Across narrative shorts and branded direction, he approaches each project as a study in image and rhythm — committed to the kind of cinema that leaves something behind after the frame ends.

narrative features, branded direction, and original scripts.
