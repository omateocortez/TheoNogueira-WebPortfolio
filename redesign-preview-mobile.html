Theo Nogueira is a director and writer based in São Paulo, working at the intersection of narrative cinema and image-led commercial work. Trained in the grammar of composition and performance.

His practice is built around the belief that the most honest cinema lives in the small gestures: the pause before a word, a hand left on a table, a room that holds more than its inhabitants.
